# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: one static HTML page at the root of Raj's GitHub Pages (raj-microsoft.github.io), self-hosted fonts, WebP thumbnails.

## Users

Raj Balakrishnan and anyone he shares the link with. They want one place that opens every public site on his GitHub.

## Product Purpose

The front door to every GitHub Pages site on raj-microsoft: the AI Web Design Library, the 12 guide-built sites, the earlier projects hub and the lab index. Success: any site is one click away, with its repo beside it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Every entry: screenshot, live link, repo link, one line on what it is.
- Excludes the private taxi repos and the Cloudrow repos, per Raj's instruction to leave them out.

## Product Principles

- The work leads; the index stays quiet.
- Nothing hidden: every link visible, no menus.
